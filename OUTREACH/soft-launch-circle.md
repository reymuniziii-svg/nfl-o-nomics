# Soft-launch circle (BUILD_PLAN Phase 4, Rey's item)

Goal: eight to twelve readers who will actually use the site for a week and tell you where
it lies, bores, or breaks. Not friends who'll say "cool." Mix:

- 3–4 **cap nerds**: people who read Over The Cap for fun, post on r/nfl or NFL Twitter about
  contracts. They'll find the method holes.
- 2–3 **ordinary fans** of specific teams (pick a Chiefs fan, a Jets fan, a Lions fan). They
  test whether the verdict strip lands in three seconds.
- 1–2 **journalists** who cover the cap or a beat (local beat writers answer DMs more than
  national ones). They test shareability.
- 1–2 **data-viz people**. They test the Field and the receipt.

What you ask them for (one message, same for all):

> I built a site that answers one question about every NFL team since 1999: what did the
> wins cost. It's free and public, and I want it beaten up before I tell anyone else.
> nfl-o-nomics.vercel.app
>
> Three asks, ten minutes total:
> 1. Open your team's current page. Tell me the first thing you didn't believe.
> 2. Share one page with someone, anywhere. Tell me if the preview card made sense.
> 3. Reply with the one thing you'd change.
>
> Replies by next Sunday. I'll send you what changed because of them.

What to collect (one row per reader in a sheet): team, what they didn't believe, the
share-card verdict, their one change, and whether they came back on their own. Five readers
saying the same thing is the backlog.

Timing: send Tuesday or Wednesday (post-MNF, pre-Thursday game). Trade deadline is
November 3, 2026; a "what the deadline cost" page is the natural second send.
