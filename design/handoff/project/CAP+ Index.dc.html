1YIELD/YARD — CAP+ Index applied to the brand system

Assumptions & reasoning

Brand applied. Same information architecture, now on the YIELD/YARD system: Film Room black dark mode over Chalk/Paper editorial surfaces, Bebas Neue display, Rajdhani for athletic labels and metrics, Inter for prose. Yield Lime is the subject and positive movement; Premium Gold marks benchmarks only — league average, the efficiency frontier, the projected cap ceiling. Red is reserved for genuine risk (dead money, falling teams).

Data is placeholder. Plausible invented figures on real team names — swap in your numbers and the layouts hold. No team logos (marks are abstract wordmark chips).

Desktop is not a stretched phone. Wide editorial dashboard: one hero read, then large charts with room to breathe, scrolling. Mobile keeps the bottom-tab terminal.

Held to the guide: one lime series per chart, gold never used as a clickable control, gridlines under 15% opacity, no gradients inside quantitative bars. CAP+ = wins produced per dollar of cap spent, indexed so 100 = league average.
