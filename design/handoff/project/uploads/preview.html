Logo rules

Consistency matters more than novelty. The logo can flex across product, editorial and social contexts, but its proportions, slash color and breathing room should remain fixed.

Clear space

YIELD/YARD

Maintain clear space equal to the width of the slash on every side.

Minimum size

YIELD/YARD

Digital: 140 px wide
Print: 1.4 in wide
Monogram: 24 px minimum

Do not

  • Change the slash to a team color.
  • Stretch, compress or italicize the wordmark.
  • Add footballs, helmets or league marks to the primary logo.
  • Use gold as the dominant wordmark color.
  • Place it on noisy photography without a dark scrim.

05

Data visualization

Charts should borrow from the field, not cosplay as one. Use yard lines as structure, lime as emphasis and gold for benchmarks or value thresholds.

Contract value trajectory · indexed

Chart rules

  • Reserve lime for the selected team, player or primary series.
  • Use gold for league average, target value or historical benchmark.
  • Keep comparison series white, gray or muted green.
  • Use field ticks only when they aid scale comprehension.
  • Avoid gradients inside quantitative marks.
  • Label the insight directly; do not make users decode a legend maze.
