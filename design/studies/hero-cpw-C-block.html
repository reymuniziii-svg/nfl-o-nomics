Study C · division cost-per-win hero

THE BLOCK

Point of view: the hero's real job is surviving as a screenshot, so design the square first and let the phone and the desktop be crops of the same logic.

What it trades away: comparison precision. Three of the four teams are demoted to a stack of 4px bars, so the division stops being a comparison and becomes a headline with footnotes. It also stakes the whole card on one number — which is fine on /division/afc-east, and wrong the moment a Jets fan opens it, because the card is about Buffalo. The subject-led variant (C4) fixes that and immediately loses the thing that made the square work: the number it leads with is no longer the interesting one.
