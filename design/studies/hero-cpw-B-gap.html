Study B · division cost-per-win hero

THE GAP

Point of view: a cost-per-win number means nothing on its own — make the league average the axis and draw each team as a distance from it.

What it trades away: absolute magnitude. You read position first and the dollar figure second, so "$18.3M per win" stops being the headline. The benchmark has to be named twice — once on the axis, once in the footer — or the card is unreadable the moment it leaves the page. And it has a failure mode the ranked list does not: a division where all four teams sit near average produces a card with almost no ink on it. The chart is only as interesting as the spread, and some divisions have none.
