Study A · division cost-per-win hero

THE RECEIPT

Point of view: the hero is a price list — rank the division by what a win actually costs, and let the ledger do the arguing.

What it trades away: the payroll. You read the ratio and never see that Buffalo and Miami spend within $6M of each other — total spend is off the card entirely. And a rank list flattens distance: 1st-to-2nd looks the same as 3rd-to-4th even when the gap is four times larger. The bar length is the only place the real spread survives, and at 390px it is 8px tall.
